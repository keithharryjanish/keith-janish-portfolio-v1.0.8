All media files below are **original user-supplied assets**. Reorganization changed paths/names only, not the files' contents.

- `public/media/profile/keith-janish.jpg`: Keith's supplied portrait.
- `public/media/profile/Keith_Janish_Resume.pdf`: Keith's supplied résumé.
- `public/media/projects/firebase/thumbnail.png`: supplied Firebase title artwork.
- `public/media/projects/firebase/trailer.mp4`: supplied Firebase trailer.
- `public/media/projects/clockwork-trials/thumbnail.png`: supplied Clockwork Trials title artwork.
- `public/media/projects/operation-station/thumbnail.png`: supplied Operation Station trailer artwork.
- `public/icons/favicon.svg`: the current cream KJ monogram with steel-blue dot on dark background (formerly v109).
- `public/icons/apple-touch-icon.png`: matching current Apple touch icon (formerly v109).
- `public/favicon.ico`: current favicon ICO, retained for browser fallback. Duplicate and obsolete icons were removed.
- Google Fonts: Barlow Condensed and DM Sans, with system font fallbacks.

Card and detail artwork fills 16:9 frames with `object-fit: cover`, preserving image proportions while cropping overflow. Operation Station's assigned video is https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9nlNoeEMFZk. Clockwork Trials has no assigned video; Firebase's MP4 is served locally.
